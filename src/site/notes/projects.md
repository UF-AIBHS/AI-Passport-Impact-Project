# 10 Health-Science ML4LLM Projects

Ten applied mini-projects for the [UF AI Passport for Health Science](https://prismap.medicine.ufl.edu/research/ai-passport/), each adapting a mechanistic-interpretability technique from [Mike X Cohen's *50 ML projects to understand LLMs*](https://github.com/saurabhr/ML4LLM_book) to open, PHI-free health-science text. No fine-tuning, no APIs — every project treats an LLM's tokens, embeddings, attention, and hidden states as data to analyze and visualize.

1. [[project-01-tokenizer-jargon|Tokenizer stress-test on medical jargon]] — Ch.2 · proj1
2. [[project-02-verbosity-drift|Verbosity drift across note types]] — Ch.2 · proj2
3. [[project-03-topic-drift-chat|Topic drift in patient–doctor chat]] — Ch.3 · proj9
4. [[project-04-drug-label-clusters|Drug-label embedding clusters]] — Ch.3 · proj8
5. [[project-05-perplexity-clinical|Perplexity of clinical language]] — Ch.4 · proj20
6. [[project-06-layer-diagnosis|Which layer "knows" the diagnosis]] — Ch.5 · proj27
7. [[project-07-attention-symptoms|Attention to symptom keywords]] — Ch.6 · proj35
8. [[project-08-radiology-position|Structural position of radiology findings]] — Ch.7 · proj45
9. [[project-09-health-literacy-gap|Health-literacy gap as embedding distance]] — Ch.3 · proj13
10. [[project-10-capstone|Capstone: full mechanism audit]] — combines 1, 3, 7, 6

All datasets are openly downloadable with no data-use agreement, so nothing blocks a 2-week cohort on credentialing paperwork.
